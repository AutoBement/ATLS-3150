A MORE INCLUSIVE WEB

Good design considers everyone.

People interact with websites in different ways. Some use screen readers, some navigate with a keyboard, and others need captions, larger text, or simpler layouts.

Universal Design encourages us to consider these differences from the beginning rather than treating accessibility as an afterthought.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Accessibility is not an extra. It is part of good design.

A better web is one where people can participate, understand information, and complete tasks regardless of their abilities or circumstances. Designing for human differences makes digital experiences stronger for everyone.
